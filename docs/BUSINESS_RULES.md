# Business Rules

1. Net Sales = Gross Sales - Discount Value.
2. Gross Margin = Net Sales - Cost Value.
3. E-commerce channel is explicitly separated from physical-store sales.
4. Promotions are analyzed descriptively; no causal uplift claim is made without a valid counterfactual design.
5. Days of Coverage uses trailing unit velocity and latest inventory snapshot.
6. OOS, overstock, dead-stock and near-expiry thresholds are configurable business rules.
7. ABC classification uses cumulative sales contribution, not row rank alone.
8. Supplier performance combines fill rate and actual lead time; commercial importance should be reviewed alongside service risk.
9. Assortment actions are recommendations for review, not automated deletion decisions.
10. Fixed transformations belong upstream; interactive/filter-aware measures belong in DAX.
